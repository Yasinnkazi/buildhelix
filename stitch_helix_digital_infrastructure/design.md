# Helix Cinematic System

A high-fidelity, cinematic design system for a modern digital infrastructure and automation brand. The system emphasizes depth, technical precision, and an "expensive" minimal aesthetic.

## 1. Brand Identity
**Vision**: Modern digital infrastructure and systems thinking.
**Personality**: Cinematic, Futuristic, Premium, Immersive, Technical, High-Trust.
**Tone**: Editorial, visionary, and startup-focused.

## 2. Visual Foundation

### Color Palette (Dark Mode - Default)
- **Background**: `#0A0A0A` (Deep Matte Black)
- **Surface**: `#121414` (Deep Graphite)
- **Surface Dim**: `#1a1c1c`
- **Primary/Accent**: `#8B5CF6` (Electric Violet)
- **Secondary**: `#4F46E5` (Indigo Glow)
- **Typography (On-Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Typography (On-Surface-Variant)**: `#94A3B8` (Soft Gray)

### Typography
- **Headlines**: *Clash Display* (or Satoshi/General Sans) - Bold, massive, futuristic. High-impact letter spacing.
- **Body**: *Chivo* / *Inter* / *Satoshi* - Elegant, clean, highly readable.
- **Editorial Accents**: Occasional italics for emphasis and a premium feel.

### Surface & Depth
- **Glassmorphism**: Liquid-glass effects with high blur (20px+) and 10% opacity white borders.
- **Gradients**: Cinematic, subtle indigo-to-violet glows. No high-saturation rainbow gradients.
- **Roundness**: `ROUND_FULL` for navbars and primary buttons; refined `ROUND_XL` for content panels.

## 3. Motion & Interaction
- **Philosophy**: Smooth, restrained, expensive.
- **Key Patterns**:
  - Reveal animations on scroll.
  - Magnetic hover effects for CTA buttons.
  - Sticky-scroll scaling for project showcases.
  - Parallax depth on UI panels.

## 4. Shared Components

### Navigation (TopAppBar)
- **Layout**: Floating pill-shaped glassmorphism bar.
- **Interactions**: Liquid-glass blur, theme toggle switch, magnetic "Book a Call" CTA.

### Footer
- **Layout**: Immersive, multi-column with high-contrast brand mark.
- **Content**: HELIX logo, social (Instagram, X, GitHub), contact (buildhelix@gmail.com).

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Electric Violet gradient with glow and reveal transitions.
- **Secondary**: Glassmorphic border with subtle hover lift.

## 5. Design Principles
- **Fidelity over Decoration**: Every element must serve a functional or architectural purpose.
- **Cinematic Spacing**: Generous whitespace to allow "massive" typography to breathe.
- **Technical Precision**: Visible grid hints, line-thin borders, and crisp iconography (Lucide).
